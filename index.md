<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

<p><strong>Effective Date:</strong> July 2026</p>

<p>
HAND Recall ("the App") is developed and published by
<strong>HAND Apps</strong>.
Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains
how the App handles your information.
</p>

<h2>1. Introduction</h2>

<p>
HAND Recall is designed with a privacy-first approach.
Most of your information stays on your own device and is not
uploaded to our servers.
</p>

<h2>2. Information We Collect</h2>

<p>
HAND Apps does not require you to create an account.
</p>

<p>
HAND Apps does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.
</p>

<h2>3. Photos and Media</h2>

<p>
If you choose to attach photos to your stored items,
those images remain on your device.
HAND Apps does not upload your photos to any server.
</p>

<h2>4. Local Storage</h2>

<p>
Items, boxes, recalls, notes, and application settings
are stored locally on your device.
</p>

<h2>5. Backup and Restore</h2>

<p>
The application allows you to export and restore your data
using local backup files.
These backup files are created and managed only by you.
HAND Apps does not receive copies of these backups.
</p>

<h2>6. Notifications</h2>

<p>
The application may use local notifications
to remind you about your saved recalls.
These notifications are generated locally on your device.
</p>

<h2>7. Advertising</h2>

<p>
HAND Recall displays advertisements provided by
Google AdMob.
</p>

<p>
Google may collect certain information to provide,
personalize, and measure advertisements according to
its own Privacy Policy.
</p>

<p>
HAND Apps does not collect, receive, store,
or have access to the advertising data collected by Google.
</p>

<p>
For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:
</p>

<p>
https://policies.google.com/privacy
</p>

<h2>8. Data Security</h2>

<p>
Because your information is stored locally,
you remain responsible for protecting your device
and your exported backup files.
</p>

<h2>9. Children's Privacy</h2>

<p>
HAND Recall is not specifically directed toward children under 13.
HAND Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
</p>

<h2>10. Changes to This Privacy Policy</h2>

<p>
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
</p>

please contact:
